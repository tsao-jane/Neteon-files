最重要的一點

真正的新手,沒出現在這份問卷裡

我們相信公司裡其實有更多新手,但他們對 AI 還沒興趣,所以也沒填問卷。結果填答的人偏向已經很上手的同事。換句話說,這份問卷代表的是「已經願意用的人」,不是全公司——而那些沒填的人,正是這個活動最想帶動的對象。

「我每天都在用 AI —— 但我從沒上傳過檔案、沒設過 Project,我也不覺得這個比賽是為我辦的。」

會場裡很可能有很多這種人 —— 由一份真實回覆還原。連「有填問卷」的人都這樣想,沒填的人更是如此。

Round 1
上手
第 1 階主秀 · 一封你每週要寫好幾次的開發信
帶到的功能:一般對話 → 多輪對話 → Projects

把每天都在寫的開發信,交給 AI

目標:讓零基礎的業務當場做出一封能用的信,並願意再來。

現場流程
  1. 先「隨便問」:貼「幫我寫封開發信」,給出空泛、罐頭味很重的版本。
  2. 換成公式 —— 角色(Neteon 工業電腦業務)+ 背景(對象是工廠自動化整合商、產品 Nuvo-XXXX)+ 任務(第一封開發信)+ 格式(英文、簡短、一個明確 CTA)。同一件事立刻變得能用,當場對比。
  3. 多輪修:請它「再短一點、加一句點出對方痛點、語氣再像人一點」,示範來回調。
  4. 開一個 Project「我的業務桌」:上傳產品線卡 + 兩三封你寫過最好的信(你的語氣)+ ICP 筆記,設自訂指令「你是我的業務助理,用我的語氣、美國 B2B 口吻」。之後只打「幫我對 ___ 公司寫第一封信」就好。
關鍵台詞

「一般對話是用完即丟;Project 是它記得你的產品、你的語氣 —— 不用每次重貼。」

進階加碼(給已經會的人)

把 Project 串上第二個檔(價目表),30 秒帶過,當成「你的下一步」。

帶走

每位業務當天建好自己的「我的業務桌」Project,並抄走那條提示詞公式。

對到 業務最常用「查資料 / 寫文案」+ Q5「customer outreach」「調對 prompt」
Round 2
瀏覽器 · Cowork
第 2 階主秀 · 讓 Claude 在你的瀏覽器裡幫你找 / 讀資料
帶到的功能:Claude in Chrome(reach:你的瀏覽器)

比規格、查客戶:從你複製貼上,到它直接讀頁面

目標:這就是 cowork —— Claude 進到你打開的網頁裡幫你做事。這場大家動手跟著裝、跟著做。

現場 ① 先教安裝(3–5 分)
  1. 用 Chrome 或 Edge(Brave / Arc 不支援)。
  2. Chrome 線上應用程式商店搜尋「Claude in Chrome」→ 加到 Chrome(或從 claude.com/claude-for-chrome 進)。
  3. 用 Claude 帳號登入(要付費方案才有功能;先確認現場誰能跟著裝)。
  4. 點工具列拼圖圖示 → 釘選 Claude;之後一鍵開側欄。
  5. 第一次會要權限(讀網頁、控制瀏覽器)→ 選「動作前先問」。

安全一句:它能幫你點、打字、截圖 —— 只在公開頁面示範,別在登入了內部系統、或有客戶個資的分頁上跑。

現場 ② 任務:讀競品頁、比 Neteon(8–10 分)
  1. 打開一個公開的競品 / 供應商工業電腦產品頁,點開側欄。
  2. 讓它直接讀你螢幕上這一頁抽規格、做表 —— 你完全沒複製貼上。
  3. 再開 Neteon 對應型號頁,做並排比較、標出 Neteon 贏在哪。
逐字指令(直接貼)

在競品頁:讀這一頁的產品規格,整理成表格 —— 欄位:型號、CPU、記憶體、儲存、工作溫度、電源、I/O、擴充槽、認證;頁面沒寫的標「未列」,不要猜。

切到 Neteon〔Nuvo-XXXX〕頁:這頁是 Neteon〔Nuvo-XXXX〕。用同樣欄位跟剛剛那台做並排比較,最後一列用一句話點出 Neteon 的優勢。

收尾:給我一份能貼進 email 的純文字比較表,再加兩句給美國製造業客戶的銷售重點,語氣專業、別誇大。

關鍵台詞

「注意,我不是在側欄打字問它 —— 我是讓它去讀我打開的這一頁。貼進聊天框只是 chat;它去讀、去動我打開的頁面,才是 cowork。」

帶走 / 卡住怎麼辦

帶走:裝好 Claude in Chrome,挑一個公開頁讓它「讀這頁 → 摘三點」。卡住:退回「截圖 / 存 PDF 上傳」的 chat 版,順便講差別。

Cowork 主秀 · 付費方案 · 限 Chrome / Edge · Team/Enterprise 可能被管理員關閉,先確認
Round 3
你的電腦 · 天花板
第 3 階主秀 · 讓 Claude 連進你的電腦,抓資料 + 存成檔案
帶到的功能:Claude 桌面版 + 檔案系統連接器(reach:你的電腦)

它不只告訴你,還把成品存進你的電腦

目標:看天花板 —— reach 從瀏覽器再進一步到你的電腦:它抓完資料,直接幫你存成檔案。由你(或預錄)示範,不要求全場安裝。

事前(只有你要設定)

用 Claude 桌面版 接上檔案系統連接器(MCP),只授權一個指定資料夾(別開放整台電腦)。這比裝 Chrome 擴充更進階、權限更大(它會寫你的檔案)。

現場流程
  1. 承接 Round 2:把幾個競品 / 潛在客戶的資料,讓 Claude 整併成一張比較表 / 名單。
  2. 下指令讓它存成檔案(例如 .csv 或 .md)到你授權的資料夾。
  3. 打開那個資料夾 —— 檔案真的在你電腦裡了,不是只停在對話框。
逐字指令(直接貼)

把這份比較表存成 CSV,檔名 neteon-vs-competitor.csv,放到我授權的那個資料夾;存好後告訴我完整路徑。

關鍵台詞

「Round 2 它出去幫我讀到資料;Round 3 它把資料帶回來、寫成我電腦上的檔案。reach 一路從聊天框 → 瀏覽器 → 我的電腦。」

怎麼演

這階是天花板:給大家看「原來可以到這」,不要求當場跟上。權限大 —— 只授權單一資料夾、用非機密的範例資料。

Claude 桌面版 + 檔案系統 MCP · 權限較大,只授權指定資料夾

適合的示範題材

  • 業務開發:研究名單、寫第一封信(業務)
  • 客戶 RFQ / 報價:對規格、出比較表(業務)
  • 會議紀錄整理與跟進信(業務 / 全體)
  • 產品資料與型錄文案(行銷)
  • 每日排程 / 出貨規劃(營運)

該避開的題材

  • 任何偏寫程式的東西 —— 沒人提出這需求
  • 會強化「這是給工程師的」這種距離感
  • 沒接到真實任務的抽象功能導覽
一句話收尾:代理人負責激發嚮往、留住進階的人;一個快速、貼近工作、 明天就能做的小成果,才是真正把新手拉進門的關鍵。兩件事每一場都要有。

A few observations

What we read from the numbers

Who to design for

Sales is the majority

6 of 9 responses are Sales, 2 Marketing, 1 Operations. Sales-flavoured demo examples land with most of the room.

Skill spread

Bimodal, not a smooth curve

3 people have used none of the deeper features; another group already uses Projects and file upload. Little in between.

Want vs. use

Agents are the biggest gap

Agents / multi-step is the most-wanted topic, but only one person has used one. The most-wanted and least-experienced area.

Confidence

Average 3.8 — read with caution

Two who rated themselves "5" have barely used AI. Confidence and actual ability don't always line up.

The most important point

The real beginners aren't in this survey

We believe there are many more beginners in the company — but they aren't interested in AI yet, so they didn't fill in the survey. The people who did respond skew toward the already-capable. In other words, this survey represents "the people already willing to use AI," not the whole company — and the people who didn't answer are exactly the ones this event most wants to move.

"I use AI every day — but I've never uploaded a file, never set up a Project, and I don't think the competition is for me."

The room probably has many of these — reconstructed from one real response. If even a survey responder feels this way, the non-responders feel it more.

The demos are run live by the organizer and recorded to video — so anyone who missed it or wants a refresher can watch later. Rung 1 main act · The outreach email you write several times a week

Hand the email you write every week to AI

Round 2
Browser · Cowork
Rung 2 main act · Let Claude find / read data inside your own browser
Feature introduced: Claude in Chrome (reach: your browser)

Compare specs, research customers: from copy-paste to it reading the page

Goal: this is cowork — Claude acting inside the page you've opened. This is the session everyone installs and follows along.

Live ① teach the install (3–5 min)
  1. Use Chrome or Edge (Brave / Arc not supported).
  2. Search the Chrome Web Store for "Claude in Chrome" → Add to Chrome (or via claude.com/claude-for-chrome).
  3. Sign in with your Claude account (features need a paid plan; check who in the room can follow along).
  4. Click the puzzle-piece icon → pin Claude; one click opens the side panel.
  5. First run asks for permissions (read pages, control the browser) → choose "Ask before acting."

One safety line: it can click, type and screenshot — so demo on public pages only, never on tabs logged into internal systems or holding customer data.

Live ② task: read a competitor page, compare to Neteon (8–10 min)
  1. Open a public competitor / supplier industrial-PC product page; open the side panel.
  2. Have it read the page on your screen and pull the specs into a table — you copy-pasted nothing.
  3. Open Neteon's matching model page; build a side-by-side comparison flagging where Neteon wins.
Verbatim prompts (paste as-is)

On the competitor page: Read this page's product specs into a table — columns: model, CPU, memory, storage, operating temperature, power input, I/O, expansion, certifications; mark anything not listed as "not listed," don't guess.

On Neteon's [Nuvo-XXXX] page: This page is Neteon [Nuvo-XXXX]. Using the same columns, build a side-by-side comparison with the previous unit; add a final row naming Neteon's edge in one line.

To finish: Give me a plain-text version of the comparison I can paste into an email, plus two sales talking points for a US manufacturing customer — professional, no hype.

Key line

"Notice — I'm not just typing in the side panel; I'm letting it read the page I've opened. Pasting into chat is just chat; it reading and acting on the page you've opened is what makes it cowork."

Leave with / if it stalls

Leave with: install Claude in Chrome, pick a public page, ask it to "read this page → summarise in three points." If it stalls: fall back to the chat version (screenshot / save as PDF and upload), and explain the difference.

Round 3
Your computer · ceiling
Rung 3 main act · Let Claude reach into your computer — grab data + save it as a file
Feature introduced: Claude desktop + filesystem connector (reach: your computer)

It doesn't just tell you — it saves the result onto your computer

Goal: the ceiling — reach goes one step past the browser, into your computer: it gathers the data and saves it as a file for you. You (or a recording) demo it; don't ask everyone to install it.

Before (only you set this up)

Connect the Claude desktop app to a filesystem connector (MCP), granting access to one specific folder (not your whole computer). This is more advanced than the Chrome extension and carries bigger permissions (it writes your files).

On screen
  1. Carry on from Round 2: have Claude consolidate a few competitors / prospects into one comparison table / list.
