So what is Cowork?

You hand it a job. It works through the steps. You get files back.

Chat

You ask, it answers.

Claude in Chrome

It reads the page you already have open.

Cowork

It does the job and hands you the result.

What's different about it
1

It works on your computer. Reads and writes your files, no uploading and downloading.

2

What comes out is a real file. Excel with formulas that work, PowerPoint, Word — not a wall of text in a chat window.

3

It runs multiple steps on its own. Describe the outcome, come back to the result.

4

It pairs with Claude in Chrome — last session's browser skill, working inside this.

Before we go further

It still needs you for four things.

A goal

It doesn't decide what's worth doing.

The data

It can only work from what you give it access to.

Permission

Every system it touches, you approved.

Your review

What it produces is a first version, not a final one.

It's handing over a job, not asking a question.

When to reach for it

Is there a file at either end?

Open Cowork

  • It starts from files you already have
  • What you need back is a file, not an answer
  • You'd be opening several files to do it by hand

Just use Chat

  • You want an answer or a second opinion
  • Nothing goes in, nothing comes out
  • You just need to know something

If the answer is “just send me the file,” that's a Cowork job.

Before you use anything it makes

How to check what it hands you.

  • Did it actually use the material I gave it?
  • Did it flag what it wasn't sure about?
  • Can I tell the facts apart from the assumptions?
  • Have I checked the numbers, specs and part codes against the source?
  • Could this go out as-is, or what still needs changing?

It can prepare the work. It can't be accountable for it.

Attachment one

Skills

A prompt explains the job once. A skill saves the method you would otherwise repeat.

A job I repeat

Every time a quote request arrives, I follow the same review and drafting process.

What changes each time

The customer, part numbers, quantities, approved price and availability.

The reusable method — this is the skill

Quote email first draft

  • Open with the part number and quantity they asked for
  • Insert the approved price, lead time and MOQ — in that order
  • If something is out of stock, say so in the first line. Don't bury it
  • Never quote freight unless they asked for it

Claude follows the approved structure. You approve the price and send.

Skill or Cowork project?

One is the how. One is the where.

A skill  ·  the how

  • Your method, written down once
  • Travels — any chat, any project, any week
  • Contains the method, not the job files

A Cowork project  ·  the where

  • Your folders, and where the finished file lands
  • Reads your files without you uploading them
  • Remembers this job from one run to the next

Two separate questions, and the answer is usually yes to both. Is there a file at either end? → project. Is the method the same every time? → skill. The skill brings the method into the project.

Why skills scale

The method stops living in one person’s head.

1

Capture it. Turn the repeatable decisions, order and guardrails into one reusable method.

2

Reuse it. Claude can apply the same method in Chat or Cowork when that kind of job comes up again.

3

Share it. When sharing is enabled, teammates can work from the same approved method.

Build the method once. Reuse it wherever the work happens.

How to create your first Skill

Two ways. Neither is technical.

A  ·  Talk it through

In any chat, describe a task you do well — “here's how I always do this.” Claude asks the questions and builds it into a proper skill file.

B  ·  Record yourself doing it

In Cowork: + → Record a skill. Do the job, narrating your decisions and exceptions. Click Done, expand Content to check it.

You never write the file. Either way, you review it before it saves.

And you never launch a skill. Ask for the job and Claude applies it when it fits. Want to be sure? Name it in your request.

Record a skill is currently available in Cowork on Claude for Mac for Pro, Max and Team plans. Talking it through works anywhere.
