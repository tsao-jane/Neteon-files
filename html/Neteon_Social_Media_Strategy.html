CHAPTER 01

Strategy Overview策略總覽

Neteon doesn't sell impulse purchases. We sell rugged, mission-critical infrastructure into 6–18 month engineering cycles. Social media's job is not to go viral — it is to be present, credible, and technically right at every step a specifier takes from "I have a problem" to "send me a quote."

Neteon 銷售的是進入 6–18 個月工程週期的關鍵任務基礎設施。社群的任務不是爆紅，而是在規格制定者從「我有問題」走到「請報價」的每一步，都保持在場、可信、技術正確。

All roads lead to the RFQ and the Knowledge Hub. 所有路徑都通向詢價（RFQ）與知識中心。每一則貼文、影片與電子報，最終都導向一個可被追蹤的工程轉換。

  • Showcase "Industrial Computing": ~27 followers

  • Specifiers research silently for months before contact
  • Competitors (Moxa, Belden, Cisco IE) own the conversation
  • Technical buyers trust demos & data, not slogans
  • AI search now shapes the shortlist before you're called
The play · 對策

Our response

  • Make LinkedIn the industrial authority feed
  • Build a video proof library on YouTube
  • Turn the Knowledge Hub into a citation magnet
  • Re-activate the installed base by newsletter
  • Give every asset a traceable CTA

CHAPTER 03

Personas目標受眾

We write for three buyers who almost never share a job title — but who all touch the same purchase order.

Persona 01 · Specifier

The SI / EPC Engineer

系統整合商／EPC 工程師
  • Role: Designs & specs OT networks for energy, rail, oil & gas projects
  • Pain: Reliability at −40°C / in hazardous zones; lead times that wreck schedules
  • Pain: OT/IT convergence and IEC 62443 compliance pressure
  • Trigger: A new tender, a failed legacy switch, a cyber-audit finding
  • Wins on: Spec sheets, ruggedness proof, application diagrams
Persona 02 · Builder

The OEM Product Engineer

OEM 產品工程師
  • Role: Embeds industrial computing / edge AI into their own machines
  • Pain: Long-term part availability & revision control
  • Pain: Fitting Jetson / Core Ultra compute into a fanless, certified box
  • Trigger: A new product line, an edge-AI feature roadmap
  • Wins on: Benchmarks, thermal data, design-in support, roadmaps
Persona 03 · Owner

The Asset Owner / Ops Lead

資產擁有者／營運主管
  • Role: Runs the plant, substation, BESS site, or data center
  • Pain: Uptime, OT security blind spots, remote monitoring
  • Pain: Justifying CapEx; fear of unplanned downtime
  • Trigger: An outage, an expansion, a security mandate
  • Wins on: Outcome stories, ROI / TCO, peer case studies

Secondary audiences: distributors / resellers (channel enablement) and government / defense procurement (compliance & provenance) — served with the same content, reframed for resale or audit.

CHAPTER 04

Voice & Messaging語調與訊息

Engineers can smell marketing a mile away. We sound like a senior field application engineer who respects the reader's time — precise, proof-backed, never hype.

Three core messages · 三大核心訊息
  • Built for where it breaks. Hardened for the heat, dust, vibration and voltage that kill commercial gear. 為極端環境而生。
  • Expertise you can call. 20+ years, 1M+ devices — real FAEs, fast turnaround, design-in support. 可以打電話找到的專業。
  • From the floor to the AI data center. One partner to collect, convert & network critical operational data. 從產線到 AI 資料中心，一站串接。
Tone dials · 語調設定
  • Technical, not academic — depth, but readable in 30 seconds
  • Confident, not boastful — let the spec and the outcome talk
  • Helpful first — teach the problem before naming the product
  • Specific — model numbers, °C, Gbps, IEC standards, real sites
Do · 應該
  • "This M12 switch held link at −40°C through a 6-month substation pilot."
  • Lead with the failure mode the reader fears
  • Show wiring diagrams, racks, and real deployments
  • Cite standards: IEC 62443, IEEE 1613, ATEX / UL Class I Div 2
Don't · 避免
  • "Revolutionize your business with cutting-edge synergy!"
  • Emoji-stuffed hype or stock-photo handshakes
  • Feature lists with no application context
  • Claims with no number, standard, or real site behind them

CHAPTER 09

The Repurpose Engine內容再利用引擎

One serious asset feeds every channel. We never create single-use content — we create a source, then atomize it.

”

Every pillar page or video becomes nine or more social assets. 每一篇支柱文章或一支影片，都拆解成 9 件以上的社群素材。

Source · 來源

One application case study

  • A deployed project (e.g. microgrid control)
  • Problem → solution → measured outcome
  • Customer-approved, with real data & diagrams
Atomize · 拆解

Becomes…

  • 2 LinkedIn posts (story + lesson)
  • 1 carousel (the diagram, step by step)
  • 1 YouTube walkthrough + 2 short clips
  • 1 X thread · 1 newsletter feature
  • 1 Knowledge Hub case page (GEO-optimized)
Compound · 複利

Why it works

  • Specifiers need 5–7 touches before they reach out
  • One case, seen across channels, becomes the touches
  • The Hub page earns search + AI citations for years
  • Cuts production load — quality over volume
CHAPTER 10

Engagement SOP互動標準流程

In B2B, the comment section and DMs are where pipeline actually starts. Thirty focused minutes a day, every weekday.

Daily 30-min routine · 每日 30 分鐘
  • 5 min — Reply to every comment on our posts, same day
  • 15 min — Comment with substance on 10 target accounts (SIs, OEMs, partners, industry voices)
  • 5 min — Reshare 1 relevant industry item + add Neteon's expert POV
  • 5 min — Open a conversation with 2–3 new relevant followers (no pitch)
DM → RFQ handoff · 私訊轉商機
  • Qualify softly: application, environment, timeline, volume
  • Lead with help — share the right guide or spec first
  • When intent is clear, route to FAE / sales with full context
  • Log every qualified DM in CRM (HubSpot); tag source = social
  • Never let a named-account question sit beyond 4 business hours

Principle: engagement is sales prospecting that doesn't feel like prospecting. Be the most helpful expert in the thread.

CHAPTER 11

Measurement Cadence衡量節奏

What gets reviewed gets improved. Three loops: weekly pulse, monthly deep-dive, quarterly steering.

Weekly · 每週

Pulse — 15 min

  • Top & bottom post by engagement
  • Follower delta per channel
  • New qualified DMs logged
  • One thing to double down on next week
Monthly · 每月

Deep-dive — 60 min

  • Funnel: impressions → engagement → RFQ
  • Which pillar & channel drove pipeline
  • YouTube view-through & Hub traffic / citations
  • Newsletter open / click trends
Quarterly · 每季

Steering

  • North Star: RFQs vs. the 40 target
  • Re-set goals & budget allocation
  • Kill or scale the X experiment
  • Publish the flagship whitepaper
Attribution that survives a long cycle · 長週期歸因
  • UTM-tag every social → site link; dedicated landing pages per campaign
  • "How did you hear about us?" field on every RFQ / contact form
  • HubSpot: first-touch + last-touch source on each deal
  • Accept that social is often the silent first touch — credit assisted pipeline, not just last-click

90-Day Roadmap90 天路線圖

Three 30-day phases: build the foundation, scale reach, then convert. No phase starts before the previous one is real.

01
基礎建立 · Foundation
Days 1–30

Get the house in order

  • Refresh LinkedIn company + Showcase (banner, about, CTA, pinned post)
  • Activate dual-track: company + 1–2 leadership voices
  • Define the visual system (colors, templates, carousel kit)
  • Batch-produce 20 baseline assets across pillars
  • Launch lead magnet #1: "Industrial Network Selection Guide" (gated)
  • Stand up YouTube + first 2 demo videos
02
擴大觸及 · Scale
Days 31–60

Build authority & reach

  • Lock in weekly application-story cadence
  • Ship 4 Knowledge Hub pillar pages (GEO / AEO-optimized)
  • Weekly YouTube + short clips repurposed everywhere
  • 2–3 partner / KOL collaborations (Moxa, Neousys, industry voices)
  • Launch "The Signal" newsletter to the installed base
  • Small LinkedIn ad test (US$1.5–3k) on the best case
03
驅動轉換 · Convert
Days 61–90

Turn attention into RFQs

  • "Free OT network / security health check" as soft sales entry
  • Retarget engaged audiences to gated whitepaper + demo CTA
  • Publish flagship: "2026 Industrial Edge & OT Networking Trends"
  • Tighten DM → RFQ handoff with sales; review attribution
  • Q-review: scale what converts, cut what doesn't, set Q3
