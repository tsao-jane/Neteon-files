Prompt formula ①

RACE — for a solid, complete ask

R
Role 角色
Who Claude should be — e.g. "a senior IPC sales engineer."
A
Action 行動
The exact task — e.g. "draft a reply to this inquiry."
C
Context 背景
Background & why — who we are, which vendors, what the customer sent.
E
Expectation 期望
Show the desired output: Asked / Missing / Reply. Verify specs, flag unknowns, and ask only quote-blocking questions.
Prompt formula ②

RTF — the simplest all-purpose one

R
Role 角色
Set who the AI is.
T
Task 任務
One clear instruction.
F
Format 格式
How to lay it out — email, table, bullet list, Markdown.

② The prompt that works — email or form
You are a sales support assistant for Neteon, a US-based industrial computing and Edge AI distributor (ipc.neteon.net). We carry products from Moxa, Neousys, ASUS IoT, Advantech, Cincoze, Innodisk, Teltonika, PLANET, Sena, ATOP, Vertiv, and Dinspace. Here's an inbound customer inquiry. Follow this sequence: 1. List what they explicitly asked for. Do not infer needs they did not state. 2. List what's missing before we can recommend a specific model or quote: environment, camera/sensor type and count, AI workload, quantity, budget, and timeline. 3. Draft a friendly, concrete reply. Recommend one sensible starting model from our vendor range, suggest a step-up model if the workload may be heavier, and close with 3-4 questions needed to finalize model and quote. Rules: Verify every product/spec claim against a vendor datasheet or Neteon product page. If you cannot verify it, say "I want to confirm..." instead of guessing. Do not invent certifications, TOPS, temperature ranges, IP ratings, voltages, or prices. Never present competitor products as options. Sign off with: [Your Name] / Neteon / ipc.neteon.net · 1-888-908-3330 · sales@neteon.net
Now it pins the exact SKU + vendor, flags what it isn't sure of, and drafts the reply.
