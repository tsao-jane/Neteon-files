The idea

From a search box to a colleague

Most of us use AI like a search box — one question, one answer, done. That's barely scratching the surface. Over a few weeks, we want to help everyone take one comfortable step further: using AI as a hands-on helper for real work — drafting, looking things up, summarizing, organizing, planning. It's easier than it looks; you just need someone to walk you through it once.

By the end, you'll be comfortable using AI for everyday work — writing an email, drafting a customer reply, summarizing a long thread, or turning a messy file into something tidy in minutes.

alreadysaving20+ hrs a month

And it's not hypothetical — it's from our own survey. A couple of us already save 20+ hours a month with AI, and most save a few hours — mostly on everyday work: drafting, looking things up, summarising, tidying files. This event is simply about spreading those wins to everyone.

And the point isn't to crown the "best." It's that everyone feels a little more confident with AI by the end. If you're starting from zero, you're exactly who we built this for.

Where it still gets stuck

In your own words: "consolidating data from different sources," "customer outreach and time management," "managing product data" — the messy, time-eating stuff.

So across the three sessions we'll show the three things that close that gap: Projects (so AI remembers your products and your voice), Claude in Chrome (so it can read and work on a web page right alongside you), and "coworking" — handing a whole multi-step task to AI and just checking the result.

How it works

Three sessions, at your pace

We'll get together three times over a few weeks. Each one is short, runs during work hours, and opens with a live demo — so you'll always pick up something new before you try it on your own work.

First

Every session opens with a live demo

At each of the three sessions we'll demo a real AI workflow on screen — from a simple version up to the more advanced stuff, so there's something for every level. Then we explore ideas together in small groups.

Then

Sign up whenever you're ready

There's no fixed deadline to register. You can sign up at the very first session and take the whole time to work on your entry — or jump in later. Totally up to you.

Last

Share once, by the final session

Hand in your one-page result any time up to the last session. You only submit once — and that final get-together is where we celebrate and hand out everything together.

In short: three sessions, a demo at each, sign up any time, submit one entry by the last session — where we wrap up and recognize everyone together.
A peek at the demos

What you'll actually see

Here's what each session actually looks like — everyday work, building up one comfortable step at a time. You don't need to know any of this beforehand; that's the whole point of coming.

Session 1 · in the chat box · Projects

The email you write every week

Watch AI draft it in your voice — then set up a one-time "desk" that remembers your products and tone, so next time it's a single line. You'll leave with your own ready to use.

Session 2 · in your browser · Claude in Chrome

Let AI read the page for you

With AI working right inside your browser, watch it read a live web page and pull out what you need — a summary, the key specs, a quick comparison — with no copy-paste at all.

Session 3 · across a whole task · coworking

Hand off the busywork

Watch AI work through several steps end to end — research, organise, even save the finished file onto your computer — while you just check the result. This is the "partner" part.

Every session ends with a real, take-it-back-to-work win — come to one, or come to all.

How to take part

Joining is easy

Just one slide

When you're ready, share a single-page slide showing what you did with AI. No long reports, ever — we just want to see your win.

On your own or together

Take part solo, or team up with colleagues — whichever feels comfortable. Both are equally welcome.

Four quick things to put on your slide

  • The task — what you were trying to get done
  • Before — how you used to do it
  • With AI — how you did it this time
  • The result — what changed (a number, or a quick before / after)
No experience needed, and you set your own pace. Just pick a real task you actually worked on — show up, give it a try, and share what you find. We'll help you the rest of the way.
