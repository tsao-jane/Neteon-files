01 · The idea

What this is about

Most of us use AI like a search box — one question, one answer, done. That's barely scratching the surface. Over a few months, we want to help everyone take one comfortable step further: using AI as a hands-on helper for real work — drafting, translating, summarizing, organizing, planning. It's easier than it looks; you just need someone to walk you through it once.

By the end, you'll be comfortable using AI for everyday work — writing an email, translating a product description, summarizing a long thread, or turning a messy file into something tidy in minutes.

A real example: writing and translating product descriptions for the catalog used to take a colleague about 2 hours for a batch. With a little AI help, it now takes around 15 minutes to draft — they just review and polish. Same result, hours back every week. Small change, real difference.

The whole point: this isn't about finding the "best." It's about everyone feeling a little more confident with AI by the end. If you're starting from zero, you're exactly who we built this for.

02 · How it works

Three sessions, your pace

We'll get together three times over the few months. Each one is short, runs during work hours, and opens with a live demo — so you'll always pick up something new before you try it on your own work.

Every session opens with a live demo

At each of the three sessions, we'll demo a real AI workflow on screen — from a simple version up to the more advanced stuff, so there's something for every level. Then we explore ideas together in small groups.

Sign up whenever you're ready

There's no fixed deadline to register. You can sign up at the very first session and take the whole time to work on your entry — or jump in later, totally up to you.

Share once, by the final session

Hand in your one-page result any time up to the last session. You only submit once — and that final get-together is where we celebrate and hand out everything together.

In short: three sessions, a demo at each, sign up any time, submit one entry by the last session — where we wrap up and recognize everyone together.

05 · How to take part

Joining is easy

SHARE

Just one slide

SOLO OR TEAM

On your own or together

Take part solo, or team up with colleagues — whichever feels comfortable. Both are equally welcome.

ON YOUR SLIDE

Four quick things to cover

To keep it simple, your one slide just needs:

  • The task — what you were trying to get done
  • Before — how you used to do it
  • With AI — how you did it this time
  • The result — what changed (a number, or a quick before / after)
No experience needed, and no deadlines to stress about. Just pick a real task you actually worked on — turn up, try a little, and share what you find. We'll help you the rest of the way.
